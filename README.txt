## Personalized guest link

Use the `to` URL parameter:

https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/?to=Nama%20Tamu

Examples:
- https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/?to=Febri%20Al%20Hafis
- https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/?to=PT%20Prima%20Sejahtera

You can also use:
https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/?to=Febri-Al-Hafis

The guest name automatically appears on the invitation and is also sent to the Google Form as "Nama Tamu/Company".

## Google Form
The RSVP form is connected to the Google Form provided for this invitation.

The guest flow is:
1. Visitor opens personalized invitation.
2. Visitor taps CONFIRM ATTENDANCE.
3. Visitor chooses YES or NO.
4. If YES, the RSVP form opens on the website.
5. Name is automatically filled and locked.
6. Visitor enters WhatsApp and guest count.
7. Submission is sent to Google Forms in the background.
8. Visitor sees the confirmation message without leaving the invitation.
